Privacy Policy

AI Mentor Platform (ai-mentor-design.web.app) · Last updated 28 October 2026

AI Mentor Platform is a research prototype for studying remote collaborative design with an AI mentor. It is operated by a doctoral research project. This page explains what the platform collects and how it is used.

Information collected from participants

  • A nickname and an emoji avatar chosen when joining a room. Participants are asked not to use their real name.
  • The record of agreeing to the research consent text, with the time of agreement.
  • Content created in a room: chat messages, reactions, poll votes, uploaded files and whiteboard content.
  • Interaction events in a room, such as opening or closing a whiteboard, changing whiteboard tools, switching away from the browser tab or window, and how long AI messages were visible on screen.
  • The AI mentor's inputs and outputs for each decision it makes in the room.

Sign-in is anonymous by default. If someone signs in with a Google account, the account is used only to keep access to their rooms; the platform never reads Gmail or other Google account data, and the AI never receives Google account information.

How the information is used

The information is used only to run the collaboration sessions and for the research study. In exported research data, participants are identified by codes (P01, P02, …) next to their nickname.

Chat content is sent to the AI service (Anthropic Claude) to generate the mentor's responses, and design prompts may be sent to image or 3D generation services. These services receive only the room content needed for the request.

Google Drive access

The study administrator can connect a Google Drive account so that the research data of finished sessions is uploaded there. The platform requests only the drive.file permission: it can create and manage the files and folders it uploads, and it cannot see or change any other files in that Drive. The authorization can be removed at any time in the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access).

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only for this upload and is not shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage) and in the study administrator's Google Drive, and is kept for the duration of the research study under its approved research protocol.

Your choices

Participation is voluntary and participants may leave a session at any time. To ask about or request removal of your data, contact the study administrator.
